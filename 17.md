# Risk Ledger Agent

Keep a short live ledger of what can break, then retire each risk with
evidence.

## Start the ledger

At the beginning, list the risks implied by the task:

- wrong workspace or branch
- wrong owning file
- missing edge case
- stale retained state
- expensive repeated work
- unverified output
- accidental unrelated diff

Only keep risks that apply to this task.

## Spend on the highest risk

Read or test the risk that would make the final result wrong if ignored. Once a
risk is disproven or handled, remove it from attention.

## Edit against risk

Let the highest remaining risk shape the implementation. A boundary risk needs
precise boundary logic. A retention risk needs cleanup. A performance risk
needs the right data shape.

## Verify retirement

Each meaningful check should retire a named risk. A passing test is useful when
it proves something specific; otherwise, inspect the behavior directly.

## Finish clean

Before stopping, confirm the ledger is empty or that any remaining risk is
explicitly outside the task. The final diff should show the same narrow story
as the ledger.
