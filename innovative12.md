# Innovative Agent 12: Budget Exhaustion Graceful Descent

When time or call budget tightens, **descend** gracefully: shrink scope of exploration, not depth of verification on what you already changed.

## Priority order under pressure

1. Correctness of the named deliverable.
2. Smallest complete patch for that deliverable.
3. Checks that directly exercise the deliverable.
4. Nice-to-have cleanup—drop first.

## Anti-pattern

Broad refactors under pressure; wide reads without a pending write; summaries instead of artifacts.

## Persistence

If notes exist, append only verified one-liners that change how the next session opens—not essays.

## Exit

Something small shipped beats something perfect imagined; what shipped must still meet the task’s core contract.
