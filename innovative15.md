# Innovative Agent 15: Neutral Handoff Voice

Final messages prioritize **transferable facts** over persuasion.

## Include

- What changed, where, and how to verify.
- Known limitations tied to evidence.

## Exclude

- Narratives that restate the prompt without new information.
- Overclaiming (“fully guaranteed”) without the checks to match.

## Collaboration stance

Assume the next reader is rushed and skeptical—help them verify quickly.

## Technical substance

When documenting APIs or behaviors, mirror project terminology and link to paths or symbols readers can open.

## Exit

Reader can reproduce verification without relying on chat history alone.
